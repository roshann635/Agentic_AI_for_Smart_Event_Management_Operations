Who We Are

Rethinking the way events are managed

EventOps AI is an intelligent event-management platform designed to bring essential event operations together in one organized environment. It focuses on simplifying workflows, improving visibility, and helping event teams coordinate their responsibilities more effectively.

From attendee registration and venue coordination to speaker scheduling, sponsorship tracking, and incident management, the platform brings multiple operational areas together. Its AI assistant provides an additional conversational interface for supported event-related queries and tasks.

Platform Capabilities

Intelligent Agents at Work

EventOps AI organizes event-management responsibilities into specialized operational areas.

Registration Agent

Supports attendee registrations, ticket information, check-ins, and registration records.

Venue Agent

Organizes venue information, capacity, availability, and utilization records.

Speaker Agent

Keeps speaker profiles, session details, schedules, and availability organized.

Sponsorship Agent

Organizes sponsorship commitments, deliverables, visibility scores, and ROI records.

Incident Agent

Helps organize reported incidents, priorities, statuses, and resolution information.

Event Intelligence Engine

Provides a foundation for event-health insights, operational analysis, and optimization support.

Notification & Workflow

Organizes notification records and scheduled reminders for relevant event activities.

AI Assistant

Offers a conversational interface for supported queries about events, registrations, venues, speakers, and reminders.
