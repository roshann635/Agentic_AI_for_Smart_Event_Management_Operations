Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how EventOps AI handles information when you use our event management platform, website, and AI-powered features.

Effective date: October 15, 2026 • Review this policy periodically
Please read this policy carefully. It describes the information the platform may process and the choices available to you. The details should be reviewed and updated to match the application's actual deployment, integrations, and data-handling practices.

1. Overview

EventOps AI is an event management platform designed to support event organization, registrations, venue coordination, speaker management, sponsorship tracking, notifications, and AI-assisted workflows.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and disclosed when you visit the website or use its features.

By using the platform, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to review this policy. Where consent is legally required for a particular activity, we will seek that consent separately.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the platform may process:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, account credentials, and account role.
  • Authentication information: Information required to verify your identity and maintain your authenticated session. Passwords should be stored as secure hashes, not as plain text.
  • Event information: Event details, registration records, attendance information, and other event-related information you provide or are authorized to manage.
  • Operational information: Venue, speaker, sponsorship, incident, reminder, and notification details entered into the platform.
  • Contact and feedback information: Information you submit through contact forms, feedback forms, or support requests.
  • AI interaction information: Prompts, questions, and related context you submit when using AI-powered features.
  • Technical information: Depending on hosting and configuration, server logs, timestamps, request metadata, browser information, and error diagnostics may be recorded.

We should collect only information that is reasonably necessary for the platform's intended functions. The exact information collected depends on the features enabled in your deployment.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and enforce access permissions.
  • Support event creation, registration, and coordination.
  • Manage venue, speaker, sponsorship, and incident records.
  • Provide notifications, reminders, and requested responses.
  • Operate AI-assisted event management features.
  • Respond to contact requests and user feedback.
  • Diagnose errors, maintain reliability, and improve security.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations where required.

Information should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or any consent required by applicable law.

4. AI Features and Automated Processing

EventOps AI may provide AI-assisted functionality using an external AI service, including Google's Gemini API, when configured and enabled by the application operator.

When you use an AI feature:

  • Your prompt and relevant context may be sent to the configured AI provider to generate a response.
  • Event information may be included in the context provided to the AI feature when necessary to answer your request.
  • Processing by an external provider is subject to that provider's applicable terms, privacy documentation, and the configuration of the service being used.
  • AI-generated responses may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable for a particular decision and should be reviewed before use.

Do not enter passwords, API keys, payment card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information into AI prompts unless the application explicitly supports that processing and appropriate safeguards are in place.

The platform operator should verify the actual AI provider, the data sent to it, applicable retention settings, and whether prompts or responses are logged before publishing this policy.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

EventOps AI does not need to make user information publicly available simply because a user has an account. Access should be limited according to the application's authorization rules and the purposes for which information is processed.

Information may be disclosed when necessary to:

  • Provide a feature you request, including an external AI integration if enabled.
  • Operate hosting, infrastructure, or other services used by the application.
  • Respond to valid legal requirements or protect the rights and safety of individuals, where permitted or required by law.
  • Support a business or operational change, where applicable law permits the disclosure.

The actual recipients of information depend on the services integrated into the deployed application. The operator should document those recipients accurately.

6. Data Security

Protecting information requires appropriate technical and organizational safeguards. These may include:

  • Secure password hashing and authentication.
  • Server-side authorization checks for protected actions.
  • HTTPS in production deployments.
  • Secure handling of API keys and environment variables.
  • Restricted access to databases and application logs.
  • Dependency updates, monitoring, and appropriate backups.

These are security measures to implement and maintain, not a guarantee that every measure is already active in every deployment. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its intended purpose, applicable operational needs, and legal obligations.

Retention periods may differ for account records, event registrations, contact requests, feedback, security logs, backups, and AI interactions.

This application does not specify a universal retention period in this policy. The operator should establish and publish appropriate retention periods based on the actual database, logging, backup, and third-party service configuration.

8. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances of processing, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information about you.
  • Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected.
  • Request deletion of information where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing where applicable.
  • Raise a privacy complaint with the appropriate authority.

To make a request, contact the platform operator using the contact details provided below. Requests may need to be verified, and some information may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it.

Specific rights and procedures depend on your location and the laws applicable to the deployment.

9. Cookies and Browser Storage

The application may use cookies or similar browser storage to support authentication sessions, maintain application functionality, and improve security.

Third-party libraries or integrations may also use browser storage depending on how they are configured. The operator should review the deployed application to identify the cookies and storage mechanisms actually in use.

Blocking essential cookies may prevent login or other features from working correctly.

10. Children's Privacy

EventOps AI is not specifically designed as a service for children. The platform operator should determine the appropriate minimum age and any consent or other requirements that apply to the intended users and jurisdiction.

If you believe that personal information has been collected inappropriately from a child, contact the platform operator so the matter can be reviewed.

11. Third-Party Services and External Links

The application may rely on third-party services for AI processing, hosting, interface libraries, or other technical functionality.

External websites and services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. EventOps AI cannot determine or guarantee how an independent service handles information outside the application's control.

Before production use, the operator should identify the relevant providers and link to their current privacy documentation where appropriate.

12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application features, integrations, data practices, or legal requirements change.

The effective date at the top of this page should be updated whenever a revised policy is published. Where required, users will be notified of material changes or asked to provide consent.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to exercise a privacy right, or have a concern about how information is handled, contact the operator of EventOps AI.
